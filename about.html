About Emmanuel

Finance, data, research, and the questions that connect them.

I am Emmanuel Olugbodi, a Finance graduate of the University of Lagos with an interdisciplinary foundation in data analytics, research, public finance, and policy analysis.

01 · The foundation

Where my professional foundation began.

My academic background in Finance has shaped the way I approach economic and organisational questions.

Over time, my interests have increasingly centred on public finance, financial management, and development-oriented policy analysis. I am particularly interested in how financial resources are managed and how government decisions affect economic outcomes.

Rather than viewing finance as an isolated discipline, I have come to see it as part of a wider system involving institutions, data, policy, governance, and development.

02 · Beyond finance

Extending financial thinking through data.

My interest in data analytics developed from a desire to strengthen the way I investigate and communicate problems.

I have developed practical skills in data analysis, visualisation, and reporting. These skills allow me to approach questions with greater empirical discipline.

I also use technical experience with digital tools to present information effectively and build practical projects that document my work.

03 · Research & policy

Research as a way of understanding complex questions.

Research writing is a central part of my professional development.

I have written analytical essays on policy, governance, and economics. My writing has received recognition in national essay competitions, strengthening my interest in research as a tool for generating practical ideas.
